## Fixed

- Power on/off now asks the bridge to keep retrying until fresh machine status confirms the requested state. A reconnect or handshake no longer causes a one-shot scheduled power request to be discarded.
- Power control remains available while the bridge is reachable and the machine reconnects. Water/grounds/status availability and caching are unchanged.
- Opposite power requests cancel pending targets; successful targets stop retrying. Drink commands are not retried.

## Updating

Update Coffee Local Bridge to 2026.11.10 or later, update Coffee Local in Marketplace, then restart Home Assistant. Pending power requests do not survive a bridge restart. Power attributes show the requested target, retry state and attempt count.
